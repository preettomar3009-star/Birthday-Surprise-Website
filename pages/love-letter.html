♥ A LETTER FROM MY HEART ♥

My Dear Kirti ❤️

Pyari Kirti,

Mujhe nahi pata ki main apni feelings ko lafzon mein kaise samjhaun, lekin main tumhe bas itna batana chahta hoon ki tum meri zindagi ke sabse khoobsurat hisson mein se ek ho.

Tumhari muskurahat mere har din ko khoobsurat bana deti hai. Tumhara saath har cheez ko khaas bana deta hai, aur tumhare saath bitaya hua har pal ek pyari si yaad ban jaata hai.

Main maanta hoon ki main perfect nahi hoon, lekin tumhare liye mere dil mein jo feelings hain, woh hamesha sachchi hain. Main bas yahi chahta hoon ki tum hamesha khush raho, muskurati raho aur apne har sapne ko poora karo.

Meri zindagi ka itna khaas hissa banne ke liye tumhara shukriya. Main chahe kitne bhi panne likh loon, phir bhi kabhi apne lafzon mein ye nahi bata paunga ki tum mere liye kitni maayne rakhti ho.

Tum meri sabse pasandeeda insaan ho. 💗

Hamesha tumhara,

Preet ❤️

♥ WORDS I WANT YOU TO KNOW ♥

My Dear Kirti ❤️

Pata nahi main apne dil ki baat tumse kaise kahun, lekin aaj main tumhe kuch aisa kehna chahta hoon jo shayad main pehle kabhi theek se nahi keh paya.

Kirti, agar meri kisi baat, kisi harkat ya kisi galti se tumhara dil dukha hai, toh mujhe sach mein maaf kar dena. Mera kabhi bhi iraada tumhe hurt karne ka nahi tha. Lekin main samajhta hoon ki kabhi-kabhi bina chahe bhi hum unhi logon ko takleef de dete hain jo hamare liye bahut khaas hote hain.

Main ye nahi kahunga ki main perfect hoon, kyunki mujhse bhi galtiyan hoti hain. Lekin main itna zaroor kehna chahta hoon ki tumhari feelings mere liye bahut important hain. Main chahta hoon ki tumhari baat samjhun, tumhari khamoshi ko bhi mehsoos karun aur apni galtiyon se behtar insaan banna seekhun.

Tumhari smile mere liye bahut special hai, Kirti. Tumhara khush rehna mere dil ko sukoon deta hai. Main nahi chahta ki meri wajah se tumhari aankhon mein kabhi aansu aayein.

Kaash main waqt ko thoda peeche le ja sakta aur apni galti sudhaar sakta. Lekin ab main sirf itna kar sakta hoon ki dil se apni galti maanun aur aage apne actions se tumhe dikhaun ki tum mere liye kitni special ho.

I am really sorry, Kirti. 🥺❤️

Main tumse turant maafi ki umeed nahi karta. Bas itni si khwahish hai ki kabhi tum mere dil ki sachchai ko samajh sako.

Tum meri zindagi ka ek bahut khoobsurat hissa ho. Aur main chahta hoon ki hamare beech hamesha pyaar, respect, understanding aur ek doosre ki muskurahat bani rahe.

Please forgive me when your heart is ready. ❤️

With lots of love,

Forever Yours, Preet ❤️
